Meet Jack

The broker who never left

Jack Klemm started selling Tracy real estate in 1988 — before Mountain House existed, before the ACE train ran, before most of today's agents had licenses. In 2008 he opened his own independent brokerage on 11th Street, and he's been the Central Valley Association of Realtors' Master's Award winner every year for 25 straight years.

No franchise. No hand-offs. Just Jack and a small team — Lisa, Mary, Alex, and Erica — who treat every deal like it's happening in their own neighborhood. Because it is.
