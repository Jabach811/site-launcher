The local read

A town first, a suburb second.

Tracy is not a subdivision pretending to be a town — it was a farm and rail town long before the commuters arrived. There is a real downtown on 10th and 11th Streets, Jack’s office among them, and established neighborhoods with mature trees that no master plan can manufacture.

Buyers here fall into two camps: Bay Area commuters trading equity for square footage, and Valley families moving up. Knowing which one is on the other side of your negotiation is half the outcome — and after nearly four thousand local closings, Jack usually knows the other agent too.
