What Jack tells people about Tracy

Tracy isn't a suburb pretending to be a town — it was a town first. There's a working downtown on 10th and 11th Streets (Jack's office included), established neighborhoods with mature trees, and new master-planned growth on the edges. Downtown-area homes move in under three weeks; brand-new construction in Tracy Hills takes longer and negotiates softer.

Buyers here split roughly into two camps: Bay Area commuters trading equity for square footage, and Valley families moving up. Knowing which camp you're negotiating against is half the game — and after nearly 4,000 local sales, Jack usually knows the other agent, too.
