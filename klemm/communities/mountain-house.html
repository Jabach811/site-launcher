The Local Read

The shortest commute in the Valley — priced accordingly

Mountain House sits right at the base of the Altamont: it's the closest San Joaquin address to the Bay Area, and the market knows it. You pay a premium over Tracy — roughly $290K at the median — for newer housing stock, K-8 village schools, and that first-exit commute.

The nuance right now: resale homes are seeing price cuts while brand-new construction keeps pulling buyers. If you're selling a resale here, pricing against the builders is the whole strategy. If you're buying, that tension is your leverage — and Jack has negotiated both sides of it.
