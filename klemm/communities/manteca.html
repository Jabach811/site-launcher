The value play with staying power

Manteca is where budgets breathe. Buyers priced out of Tracy or Mountain House find newer square footage here for meaningfully less — with Costco, Bass Pro, and the 120 corridor's retail growth filling in around them. It's also a strong move-up and investment market, which is why Jack's Manteca work spans first homes to 1031 exchanges.
