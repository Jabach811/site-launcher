The commute, honestly

Yes, there is a hill. Here is how people actually do it.

Nobody will tell you the drive does not exist. What they will not tell you is how many families never make it — because the train, or the hybrid week, changed the arithmetic.

01

The ACE train

Board in downtown Tracy or Lathrop and work, read or sleep your way to Pleasanton, Fremont or San Jose. No white knuckles on the 580.

02

I-205 to I-580

The classic run over the Altamont. Leave early, and the pass is quiet. Leave at eight, and it is not.

03

The hybrid week

Most of Jack’s Bay Area buyers now commute two or three days. That single fact has redrawn what counts as too far.
