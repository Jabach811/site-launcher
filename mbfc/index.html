Why here

An app cannot
come to your house.

You could photograph forty things, write forty descriptions, answer forty questions and ship forty boxes. Or you could hand us the bag.

We price it, photograph it, put it on the floor, put it in a live show, and hand you a check. Fourteen years of doing it for Tracy, and the same person still looks at every piece.
