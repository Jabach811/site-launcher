Four steps

How it works

No shipping labels, no photography, no answering questions from strangers at eleven at night. You hand us the bag.

  1. Check the list, then book

    Have a look at what we accept — it takes about four seconds and saves you a trip. Then book a slot. New consignors always need an appointment; after that you can drop and run.

  2. Bring up to 25 hung pieces

    Plus a bag of shoes and accessories. Everything washed or dry cleaned, on hangers, no smoke or pet hair. We go through it with you, right there, and tell you what we can take.

  3. We price, shoot and sell it

    Onto the floor, onto the website, and into the live shows we run three times a week. You can watch every piece move from your account.

  4. You get paid

    40% of the sale price. Request a check any time from your account — they go out on the 15th of the month, on balances over $20 — or spend it as store credit the same day you earn it.
