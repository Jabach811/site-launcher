Before you buy

What our grades
actually mean.

We would rather tell you about a scuff up front than argue about it later. Every consigned piece carries one of four grades, and the photos show the flaws.

Excellent

Like new

Worn once or twice, or never. No visible wear at all.

Very Good

Barely worn

Minor signs of wear — light marking or the faintest fade. Nothing you would notice across a room.

Good

Loved

Moderate wear, creasing or marking, photographed close up so you can judge it yourself.

Fair

Well loved

Real wear, priced accordingly. Rare on our floor and always described in detail.
