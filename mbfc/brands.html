The list

What we take —
and what we don’t.

This page does two jobs. If you are consigning, it tells you whether to bother packing the car. If you are shopping, every name below is a door into what we have.

The short version: if it is current, clean, and someone in Tracy would recognise the label, bring it. We take everything from Zara to Chanel — the difference is how we price it, not whether we want it.

Save yourself the trip

No, thank you

Not a judgement — just what does not sell on our floor. We would rather tell you here than at the counter with your car double-parked.

Ultra fast fashion

Shein, Temu, Fashion Nova and the like. It does not hold up on a rack and it does not hold value.

Big-box basics

Walmart, Target adult lines, Forever 21, Old Navy adult. Kids’ Target and Old Navy we will look at.

Anything worn out

Stains, holes, pilling, stretched necklines, missing buttons, scuffed soles. If you would not lend it to a friend, we cannot sell it.

Anything that smells

Smoke or pet odour will not come out on our floor, and it transfers to everything hanging next to it. Wash or dry clean first, please.

More than about two seasons old

Exceptions for luxury, denim and genuine vintage — those are timeless and we always want them.

Fakes

We authenticate everything above a few hundred dollars. If it is not right we will hand it back, no hard feelings.
