Things an app
cannot do for you.

Most of this is free, and most of it exists because somebody asked. If you want something that is not on the list, ask.

Personal shopping

Tell us the occasion, the budget and what you will not wear. We will have a rail ready when you arrive.

Product alerts

Hunting a specific bag, brand or size? We will message you the day it lands — the only sane way to shop one-of-one stock.

After-hours by appointment

The shop, to yourself, any evening. Good for a big occasion or if daytime simply does not work.

Private shopping parties

Bring your people, we will open up after close. Birthdays, bridal parties, or an excuse on a Thursday.

Ladies’ night

Regular evenings on the floor with a glass of something. Watch Instagram for the next one.

Fundraisers

We host shopping evenings for local schools and causes, with a share of the night going back to you.

Closet clearing at home

We come to your house, go through it with you, and take away what will sell. Moving, downsizing, or clearing a family member’s things.
