What we will never do: hide a flaw in a flattering photograph. Every consigned piece is shot on the same light ground, in the same three angles, plus a close-up of anything wrong with it. If we missed something, tell us and we will make it right.

If you are consigning

What we can and
cannot take in.

Our intake standard is stricter than our grading chart — because a piece has to survive twelve weeks on a rack looking as good as it did on day one.

Freshly cleaned

Washed or dry cleaned before it comes in. Not "clean enough" — actually just done.

On hangers

Hung items arrive hung. It sounds fussy; it is the difference between a ten-minute appointment and an hour.

No stains or holes

Including the ones under the arms and along the hem. We will find them, so have a look first.

No pilling

The most common reason we hand knitwear back. A fabric shaver the night before saves a lot of pieces.

No smoke or pet odour

It does not lift on our floor and it transfers to whatever hangs beside it.

Current, or timeless

Within about two seasons — unless it is luxury, denim or genuine vintage, which we always want.
