**Provisional website rating: 4/10** (not visually verified). But every structural signal points to a dated WordPress template: obfuscated email, form-based lead capture, no apparent IDX/map search, and a brand fragmented across four domains plus a stock MLS microsite. For a 35-year top-producing broker, the web presence under-represents the business — which is exactly the redesign opportunity.
