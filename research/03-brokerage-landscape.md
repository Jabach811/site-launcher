# Tracy, CA Real Estate Brokerage Landscape

**Market scope:** Tracy, Mountain House, Lathrop, Manteca (San Joaquin County, CA) · **Research date:** 2026-08-19

> **Method note:** The research environment's network policy blocked direct fetches of most brokerage websites, so findings are assembled from web search results, directory listings, and review-site data. Web presence scores are informed judgments from site structures, URL patterns, and source descriptions rather than full page renders.

**Market context in one line:** ~1,233 licensed agents compete in Tracy alone ([FastExpert](https://www.fastexpert.com/top-real-estate-agents/tracy-ca/)) — a fragmented market with no dominant local brand, where nationals hold the office signage but individual agents/teams hold the actual business.

---

## 1. The Tracy CA Housing Market (positioning context)

- **Median sale price (Tracy):** ~$673K last month, down 5.3% YoY ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/city/20144/CA/Tracy/housing-market)); Zillow's home value index reads ~$622,500, roughly flat YoY ([Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/7453/tracy-ca/)); other trackers place median sale price $670K–$760K in early 2026 ([Houzeo](https://www.houzeo.com/housing-market/california/tracy)).
- **Pace:** "Very competitive" — Redfin Compete Score 71/100; ~2 offers per home, ~31–42 days to pending citywide; Downtown Tracy sells in ~18 days while new-build-heavy Tracy Hills lingers ~112 days. Some sub-market reports show hotter conditions (homes moving in 9 days, 101% of ask, 0.33 months supply).
- **The value story:** Tracy sits ~$225K–$270K below the California median and dramatically below Tri-Valley comps — Pleasanton ~$1.7M, Dublin ~$1.2M, Livermore ~$1.1M. Buyers get far more square footage per dollar, which is why the buyer pool is heavily Bay Area professionals and families commuting over the Altamont ([Hero Real Estate market report](https://herorealestate.com/blog/tracy-ca-housing-market-report-spring-2026)).
- **Mountain House:** Now an incorporated city and one of California's fastest-growing — +5.6% population in one year (pop. ~30,687), growth driven by new construction ([Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/07/mountain-house-boom-turns-quiet-cul-de-sacs-into-a-construction-zone-7088132/)). Median sale price ~$963K (down 3.5% YoY); median list ~$888K. Resale homes are seeing price cuts while new-home sales keep drawing buyers.
- **Website positioning implication:** The winning content angle in this market is *Bay Area commuter value migration* (Tri-Valley price comparisons, commute content, new-construction guidance for Tracy Hills / Mountain House / Lathrop's River Islands) — exactly what the savvier local independents (Hero, Just 1) already blog about.

## 2. Brokerage Profiles

### 2.1 Keller Williams (KW Tracy market center)
- **Type:** National franchise. Market center at 2311-A N Tracy Blvd; high-profile KW teams operate from 60 W 10th Street (downtown). KW Tri-Valley (Pleasanton) teams also farm Tracy.
- **Footprint:** One of the two most visible national brands in Tracy proper; multiple established teams.
- **Web/tech for agents:** KW Command platform; every agent gets a templated `.kw.com` subdomain site. Corporate site modern; agent templates clean but uniform. **Web presence score: 7/10.**
- **Notable local agents/teams:** **Janet Anderson & Associates** — the standout: 5.0 stars, 137 closed sales, ~$89.4M volume ([Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/profile/janetandersonrealty)); Michelle Izaguirre; Diego J. Herra; Patrick Bill.
- **Reputation:** Individual team reviews (especially Anderson) are excellent; the office brand itself is less reviewed than its teams.

### 2.2 PMZ Real Estate
- **Type:** Regional independent powerhouse (HQ Modesto) — the Central Valley's dominant brokerage.
- **Local office:** 3101 Tracy Blvd, Tracy; sibling offices in Manteca, Stockton, Lodi, Ripon (15 residential offices total).
- **Footprint:** ~509–600+ agents company-wide; $1.2B–$2B+ annual sales; self-described #1 firm in Stanislaus, San Joaquin and Merced Counties; top-50 US mega-broker ([RealTrends](https://www.realtrends.com/brokerage-profile/pmz-real-estate-modesto-ca/)). Caveat: center of gravity is Modesto/Stockton — Tracy is an edge market for them.
- **Web/tech:** Own full IDX portal at pmz.com; agents get branded subdomains. Functional, content-rich, moderately modern. **Web presence score: 7/10.**

### 2.3 eXp Realty
- **Type:** National cloud-based brokerage (virtual model). Listed address 68 East 11th Street, Tracy.
- **Footprint:** Growing fast in commuter markets; distributed agents. Active Tracy names: **Darrin DeSilva / DeSilva Realty Group**, Soledad Garcia.
- **Web/tech:** Every agent gets a kvCore-powered `.exprealty.com` template site with IDX; recruiting pitch is revenue share + stock + cloud tools. **Web presence score: 7/10** (modern stack, zero local storefront identity).

### 2.4 Compass
- **Type:** National, design-forward. No dedicated Tracy storefront; **21 agents listed for Tracy**, mostly from Tri-Valley/Danville offices.
- **Notable local agent:** **Mike Samawi / Team Samawi** — the marquee Compass name for Tracy–Mountain House: 15-yr veteran, top producer, based in Mountain House; own branded site [teamsamawi.com](https://teamsamawi.com/).
- **Web/tech:** compass.com is arguably the best-designed national brokerage site; polished agent pages + CRM/marketing suite. **Web presence score: 9/10** on design — but local depth is thin.

### 2.5 RE/MAX (three separate Tracy franchises)
- **RE/MAX Diamond** — 2311 N Tracy Blvd Ste A; team leaders Eric & Lynda Hawkins.
- **RE/MAX Gold** — 672 W 11th St #374 (part of the large NorCal RE/MAX Gold network).
- **RE/MAX Property Professionals** — 2453 Naglee Rd.
- **Footprint:** Fragmented — three small-to-mid franchises rather than one strong office. Agents rely on remax.com corporate profile pages; local franchise sites minimal. **Web presence score: 5/10.**

### 2.6 Century 21 Select Real Estate, Inc.
- **Local office:** 2 West 11th Street, Tracy (downtown corner); **20 agents, 27 listings**; English/Spanish/Portuguese roster. Joint Manteca–Tracy operation.
- **Notable agents:** Grace Alvarez, Jennifer Wells.
- **Web/tech:** Standard century21.com corporate template profiles — presentable but cookie-cutter. **Web presence score: 6/10.**

### 2.7 Coldwell Banker Valley Central
- **Local office:** 1486 W 11th St, Tracy ([Yelp — 20 reviews](https://www.yelp.com/biz/coldwell-banker-valley-central-tracy-6)). Veteran-heavy roster (~12-yr avg tenure); also does property management.
- **Notable agents:** Harjinder Singh, Lawrence Cristobal, Kirk Bounds, Kelli Smith, Saba Kamran, Frew Tibebu.
- **Reputation:** Mixed — strong praise for several agents but pointed complaints about unresponsiveness and cold-call behavior.
- **Web/tech:** Leans on coldwellbanker.com corporate pages; no strong local identity. **Web presence score: 5/10.**

### 2.8 Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Drysdale Properties — Tracy
- **Local office:** 403 W. 11th St, Tracy; **~14 agents**.
- **Notable agents:** Graciela Canales Gottesman, Linda Castro, Jose de Lange, Frank Rocha. The office's longtime managing broker **Christine Lynch left in 2023 for Legacy Real Estate** — a meaningful defection ([RISMedia](https://www.rismedia.com/2023/04/18/christine-lynch-joins-legacy-real-estate-associates-era-powered/)).
- **Web/tech:** Template subdomain sites (`*.bhhsdp.com`) — conservative, somewhat dated. **Web presence score: 5/10.**

### 2.9 Realty ONE Group Zoom
- **Local office:** 4600 Tracy Blvd Suite 106, Tracy. 100%-commission model; recruiting pitch: "100% commission, 100% training, 100% cutting-edge tools" — attracts volume-minded and part-time agents.
- **Notable agents:** Malalee Qayumi, Rodney Som, Henry Brooks.
- **Web/tech:** Modern black-and-gold corporate branding; corporate profile pages; limited local differentiation. **Web presence score: 6/10.**

### 2.10 Home Buyers Realty (local independent — Tracy institution)
- **Type:** Local independent, est. 2000; broker/owners **Ron & Eva Cedillo**. Office: 672 W 11th St, Ste 380 (same building as Klemm).
- **Footprint/claims:** 28+ years experience, **3,500+ homes sold**, guaranteed-sale program ("we sell it or buy it"); heavy radio + social + internet marketing. Sub-team: Ben Adrian & Associates. Also runs a rentals arm (hbrrentals.com).
- **Web/tech:** Multiple sites (homebuyersrealty.com, hbrteam.com, MetroList template) — lead-gen oriented, content-heavy but visually template-driven. **Web presence score: 6/10** (strong SEO/content volume, dated design).

### 2.11 Legacy Real Estate & Associates, ERA Powered
- **Type:** Regional independent (Fremont-based, founded 1971; 5 offices, ~250 agents) expanding into Tracy.
- **Tracy presence:** Anchored by **Christine Lynch**, broker associate (licensed 1992, former BHHS Tracy managing broker, past CVAR board member) — a significant name-brand hire.
- **Web/tech:** Company IDX site with agent pages; Lynch's own ourrealestatelady.com is a dated agent-template site. **Web presence score: 6/10.**

### 2.12 Local independents & boutiques (the interesting layer)
- **Hero Real Estate** — Tracy; founded 2004 by broker/owner **Donny Piwowarski** (ex-tech, 1,000+ homes sold); publishes genuinely good Tracy market-report content that ranks in search for "Tracy housing market." Modern indie site. **Web presence score: 7/10.** ([herorealestate.com](https://herorealestate.com/))
- **Just 1 Real Estate** — Tracy; led by **Lulu Logan**, third-generation REALTOR, "Best of Zillow" team, claimed top 1% nationwide. Neighborhood guides (incl. Tracy Hills), a podcast, testimonials — the most content-forward modern local site found. **Web presence score: 8/10.** ([just1realestate.com](https://just1realestate.com/))
- **Klemm Real Estate, Inc.** — our client. Boutique but elite reputation ("by far the most professional and resourceful brokerage in Mountain House and Tracy" per reviews). Modest site. **Web presence score: 5/10.**
- **Crown Key Realty** — Tracy; sales (Michele Lazaro) + full property management arm (ckrpm.com). **Web presence score: 5/10.**
- **Go2Realty Pros / The Polgar Group** — **Gevon Polgar**, a fixture on Mountain House "best agent" lists. **Web presence score: 5/10.** ([gevonpolgar.com](https://www.gevonpolgar.com/))
- **EXIT Realty** — Shaly Dhanjal, frequently listed among Tracy's best agents; corporate-template profile.
- **Agent4Life Realty** — small local shop with Tracy seller-focused site.

## 3. Cross-Cutting Observations for the Klemm Website Project

1. **Template monoculture at the nationals.** KW, eXp, BHHS, C21, RE/MAX, Realty ONE — nearly every franchised agent in Tracy has an interchangeable template page. A genuinely custom, locally-authored site is an immediate differentiator.
2. **The best local web presences are independents** (Just 1, Hero), and they win via *content*: neighborhood guides, market reports, podcasts. Hero's market report outranks national portals for "Tracy housing market" queries.
3. **Top producers brand themselves, not their brokerage.** Janet Anderson (KW), Mike Samawi (Compass), the Cedillos (own shop), Jack Klemm — client-facing identity is the team, with the brokerage as a footnote. The new Klemm site should follow this pattern.
4. **Nobody fully owns the Mountain House growth story online** despite it being the region's biggest narrative (fastest-growing new city in CA, ~$950K medians, construction boom). Samawi, Polgar, and Klemm compete there; content leadership is still up for grabs — and Klemm already owns mountainhousere.com.
5. **No dominant brokerage.** PMZ dominates the Central Valley overall but is Modesto-centric; KW has the strongest Tracy office presence; the rest is fragmented across ~20 brokerages and 1,200+ agents.
